# Next independent harmonization experiment

Status: protocol only. No expanded data or new scientific result is claimed.

The existing 50-day result remains a frozen exploratory benchmark. Its 2025 test results have already been inspected; further model choices must not be described as blind evaluation on those dates.

Before downloading or fitting the next experiment, record a dated protocol and source-product availability. Proposed expansion: all days in 2021–2025, Ghana, compatible MODIS Aqua and VIIRS Suomi-NPP standard-processing products. Split 2021–2023 for training, 2024 for model selection and diagnostics, and 2025 for a disclosed retrospective replication. Reserve a new geographic region or later compatible period, selected before viewing its results, for an independent transfer evaluation. Product availability must be checked before fixing that final holdout.

Keep the current 0.1-degree occupied-cell scale and training-only monthly Aqua means as declared baselines. Do not tune the grid against held-out error. Report errors by month, geography and activity range as well as combined MAE/RMSE/bias. Report support and missing dates alongside every result. Do not interpret completed FIRMS requests as cloud-free observing opportunity.

Continuous returned detections alone do not establish a coverage-adjusted record. Acquire suitable swath/quality/cloud metadata and document how observing opportunity differs by sensor. Preserve original source files, checksums, product versions and UTC times. Keep NRT records separate from standard processing.

Planetary Computer scene discovery can identify candidate context imagery. An independent event review requires a documented labeling protocol, reviewers, uncertain/nonobservable cases and a geography/time split before labels influence model decisions. Weather and news cannot serve as fire-truth labels.

Acceptance: a reproducible source manifest; disjoint fit/selection/transfer sets; raw and seasonal baselines; monthly and geographic diagnostics; coverage limitations; reproducible exports; and an explicit distinction between improved sensor agreement and independently measured fire accuracy. No new accuracy or operational warning claim is authorized by this protocol.
